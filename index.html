PNP dCOPS 2026

Promotion Application Guide

Digitalized & Competency-Based Online Promotion System  ·  May 1–13, 2026

⚠️ Important Reminders

  • Qualified personnel will receive a token to access dCOPS for promotion.
  • Application window: May 1 to 13, 2026 only.
  • Download, accomplish, and upload all required documents within the window.
  • Personnel with lacking requirements will be marked Incomplete.
  • Personnel found disqualified during processing will be marked Rejected.
  • If qualified but not generated in dCOPS, inform the PMT immediately via memorandum.

📋 Requirements Checklist

ℹ️ All documents must be uploaded as individual PDF files (one file per document). Max file size: 5 MB. No watermarks or scanner app logos (e.g., CamScanner).

Step-by-Step Process

STEP 1 Log in to your OPRMS Account ▼

Use your individual OPRMS account to access the system.

✅ Use This URL
For individual personnel accounts
🔗 pais.pnp.gov.ph
❌ Do NOT Use
This is for OPRMS Admin accounts only
🔗 paisadmin.pnp.gov.ph
STEP 2 Navigate to Promotion Management ▼

After logging in to your dashboard, locate the left-side navigation menu and click "Promotion Management" to expand the sub-menu.

Then click "Apply Promotion" from the sub-menu options that appear.

✅ A notification badge may appear beside Apply Promotion if you are eligible and a token has been issued.
STEP 3 Read the Eligibility Requirements Notice ▼

A yellow notice box will appear listing all mandatory requirements. Read it carefully. These must be completed to avoid disqualification.

  • Update your IPER data in OPRMS
  • Update your PFT data in OPRMS
  • Upload your Affidavit of Undertaking
  • Encode your National Police Clearance details
  • Encode your HROASIS details
  • Update your Assignment (Diversity) data in OPRMS
  • Update your Awards and Recognition data in OPRMS
⚠️ Failure to fulfill these requirements will result in disqualification per PNP MC No. 2018-048.
STEP 4 Print the Affidavit of Undertaking ▼

Click the "Print Affidavit" button to download the DPRM-PNCOD (Revised 2025) Affidavit of Undertaking for Promotion.

📄 Print the affidavit and have it administered/notarized by your Admin Officer before clicking "Apply Promotion."
STEP 5 Accomplish the Affidavit of Undertaking ▼
  • Check all details: address, designation, and date of designation
  • Put a check mark (✓) in the appropriate boxes for items No. 8, 17, and 18
  • If no pending case: put N/A in each column of the case details table
  • If with pending case: indicate all case details accordingly
📅
Administering Period: May 1–13, 2026 only.
Authorized Officers: C, RPRMD/ARMD or Administrative Officers.
If unavailable, the designated OIC may administer the affidavit.
STEP 6 Scan the Accomplished Affidavit ▼

Scan the signed and notarized affidavit and save it as a single PDF file.

✅ APPROVED: Clean scan with no watermarks, logos, or markings from scanner apps.
❌ REJECTED: Documents with CamScanner watermarks or app logos will be rejected. Use a printer scanner whenever possible.
STEP 7 Print the Training Evaluation Form ▼

Click the "Print Training Evaluation Form" button to download the Occupational/Specialized Training Evaluation Form.

Fill it out with all relevant training/learning intervention details (title, dates, hours, conducted by), then have it signed by the Applicant for Promotion.

ℹ️ Scan and save as a single PDF before uploading in Step 11.
STEP 8 Click "Apply for Promotion" ▼

Go back to the Apply Promotion page. Click the dropdown arrow on the "Apply Promotion" button, then select "Apply Promotion" from the options.

⚠️ Ensure the Affidavit of Undertaking is already printed, accomplished, and administered by your Admin Officer before proceeding.
STEP 9 Review Your Promotion Details ▼

The promotion application form will load, displaying your personnel details:

  • Current Rank & Next Rank
  • Unit, Sub-Unit, Station
  • Date Designated, Last Promotion Date, Retirement Date
  • TIG (Time-in-Grade), Education, Eligibility, Training
  • PFT and IPER ratings

Verify all details before proceeding. Scroll down to fill in the required fields.

STEP 10 Encode Clearance & Case Details ▼
  • With Pending Criminal Case? – Click YES or NO
  • If YES: fill in Nature, Date Filed, Status of the Case
  • Attached Certification from the Court? – Click YES or NO
  • Police Clearance Date Issued – enter the date (e.g., 04/30/2026)
  • Police Clearance ID No. – enter the transaction number
  • HROASIS Certificate Control No. – enter the reference number
ℹ️ Refer to your National Police Clearance and HROASIS Certificate for the correct numbers to encode.
STEP 11 Upload Supporting Documents ▼

Click "+ Add Files" to upload your documents. After each upload, select the correct Document Type from the dropdown.

Required uploads:

Affidavit of Undertaking Training Evaluation Form Body Mass Index (BMI) Certification from Court (if applicable)
  • Each document must be a single PDF file
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB per file
  • You can drag & drop files onto the upload area
  • No CamScanner watermarks — use a printer scanner
📎 BMI submission must include 3 months of monitoring data validated by Health Service, plus the Certification from HS.
STEP 12 Submit Your Application ▼

Once all documents are uploaded and tagged with the correct document type, scroll down and click the "Submit" button.

✅ A successful submission will move your application to "For Evaluation" status.
STEP 13 Monitor Your Application Status ▼

After submission, check back regularly to monitor your status:

For Evaluation Application submitted; currently being reviewed by the dCOPS Processor.
For Interview Application evaluated and qualified. You have successfully applied for promotion.
Incomplete Correction needed. Review remarks and re-upload the corrected file (see Step 14).
Rejected Disqualification found during processing. Contact your concerned secretariat.
STEP 14 If Status is "Incomplete" – View Remarks ▼

If your status shows Incomplete, there is an issue with your submitted PDF or encoded data.

  • Click "Apply Promotion" dropdown → select "View Detail" to read the processor's remarks
  • Click "Edit" to make corrections and re-upload the corrected file
⚠️ Address the specific remarks noted by the dCOPS Processor before resubmitting.
STEP 15 Re-upload Corrected File & Resubmit ▼

In the Edit Promotion page:

  • Click the "Delete" button (🗑) next to the incorrect file
  • Upload the corrected PDF using "+ Add Files"
  • Ensure the correct Document Type is selected
  • Click "Submit" to resubmit your application
✅ After resubmission, wait for the status to update to "For Interview" — this confirms your application is complete.
